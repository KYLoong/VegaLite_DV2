Malaysia Population Dashboard (2025)

Population Density by State

Kuala Lumpur stands out as the most densely populated region as being the smallest in land area, it has the highest population concentration. Pulau Pinang follows next, with a high density driven by its urban development and limited land. States such as Selangor and Putrajaya also show significant density due to their economic and administrative importance.

Population by Ethnicity in Malaysia (2016–2025)

Malays have been the largest contributor to Malaysia's population, forming over half of the total population over the years.

Population Trend by Ethnicity (2016–2025)

Malaysia's population has shown a steady increase over time. The Malay population continues to grow steadily, while other ethnic groups remain relatively stable.
